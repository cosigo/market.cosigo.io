CIGO Whitepaper

This page is reserved for the full CIGO whitepaper.

CIGO is a separate BNB Smart Chain market-access token used for trading, liquidity, public market activity, and CIGO-specific access infrastructure. It is not the COSIGO™ silver-referenced Ethereum token, is not silver-backed, and is not a claim on COSIGO reserves or redemption inventory.
