important notice

CIGO is a speculative market-access token. Volatility, losses, liquidity shocks, and fast market movement are inherent to speculative trading. Official COSIGO remains separate and is published at token.cosigo.io.

Important notice for users, reviewers, and labelers: CIGO is more accurately described as a protected, intelligently controlled token with strong administrative and market-defense features, not by prejudicial “malicious” label applied in MetaMask-related warning flows. CIGO was designed as a speculative market-access token with protective mechanisms intended to limit bot pressure, whale concentration, and liquidity abuse during fragile stages of market formation. Those features may raise centralization concerns and deserve open review, but that is not same as a malicious designation or impairment of normal wallet operability. No specific abusive function or actual misconduct by CIGO was identified to COSIGO by two representatives involved. Both declined to remove or correct their designation and appeared to treat CIGO as though it were a scam token, despite absence of any clearly stated finding of fraud or exploit. Centralized scanners and labeling systems currently operate with broad discretion and limited accountability. Holders who acquired CIGO did so with notice that it is a controlled-risk token structure with explicit protections and restrictions. CIGO is not presented as an unsupported meme token or hype token. Its design is controlled and deliberate, and in that respect is closer to administratively managed tokens such as USDT than to an unmanaged meme asset. Secure is a fair description; scam is not. Relevant notices and disclosures are stated throughout COSIGO website. In light of third-party restrictions, opaque warning systems, and inconsistent wallet behavior, holders have been advised to approach CIGO trades carefully, review all warnings, and make informed decisions while adverse labels remain in place. COSIGO currently regards Rabby wallet as a more transparent and functional option. For further details, see labels.cosigo.io ↗.
